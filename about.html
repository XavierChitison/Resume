About me

Curiosity gave me a path into engineering.

I’ve always liked understanding how things work, taking on difficult problems, and creating something better on the other side.

My background

A builder from the beginning

For as long as I can remember, I’ve enjoyed breaking things down, rebuilding them, and solving problems. Once I learned that this is exactly what engineers do, I knew I wanted to become one.

Outside of school, I enjoy learning through technology, creating new things, and finding practical solutions to real challenges.

My journey

Turning interest into action

Exploring engineering

High school · MSOE career program

A two-week engineering program at Milwaukee School of Engineering introduced me to several disciplines. Software engineering stood out because it combined creation, problem solving, and technology.

Teaching myself to code

Independent learning

When programming courses were not available at my school, I turned to online tutorials and began learning Swift—a first step into app development and building my own ideas.

Leading a capstone project

Senior year

I developed a school loyalty rewards app, reached out to companies for guidance, and sought mentors to move the work forward. The project won a national award and was featured in the news.

Education

Currently pursuing an A.A.S. in Software Development, with plans for a bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and a master’s degree in Software Engineering.

Recognition

A full-tuition scholarship supports my education, and my capstone team received a national award for our work.

Career goals

I want to become a software engineer who builds reliable products, learns continuously, and contributes thoughtfully to a strong team.
